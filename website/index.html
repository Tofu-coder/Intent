THE PROBLEM

Computers are organized around applications.
Humans are organized around objectives.

Today, accomplishing something means navigating software, interfaces, commands, services, and devices. INTENT explores a different architecture: make the objective the organizing primitive.

ARCHITECTURE

The objective owns the computation.

Models, tools, applications, devices, and people become capabilities available to the runtime.

01

Objectives

Persistent goals, constraints, priorities, deadlines, and success conditions.

02

World State

Facts, observations, inferences, uncertainty, events, and provenance.

03

Capabilities

Models, software, APIs, devices, and human capabilities exposed to the runtime.

04

Verification

Evidence-driven evaluation of whether an objective has actually been achieved.

BUILDING IN PUBLIC

An open runtime.
An open protocol.
An open ecosystem.

INTENT is being built from the runtime upward. The goal is not another AI application. It is an open foundation for software that works around what people are trying to accomplish.
